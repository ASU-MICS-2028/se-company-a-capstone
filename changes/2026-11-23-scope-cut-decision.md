# Proposed scope cut after the proposal grade

## What changed

A proposed decision that re-ranks the proposal's MoSCoW list:
- Offline sync and serving the model live move from Must to Should.
- Learned travel time is replaced by a travel band the patient chooses.
- "Service status today" is added as a Should.
- Web join, WhatsApp, more than one clinic, real handsets and patient accounts are made explicit Won'ts.

## Why

Feasibility scored 1.5 / 2.5 ("over-scoped or under-ambitious"). Sprint 1 produced no code, and Milestone 2 is on 10 Dec.
