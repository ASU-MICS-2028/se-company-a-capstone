# S01: nurse, health centre (23 Nov 2026)

**Who:** a nurse with 5 years at this health centre. She worked at a regional hospital before.
**Where:** a community health centre in Greater Accra, with two nurses on duty and one patient present.
**How:** in person, in English and Twi, about 11 minutes. Recorded with consent asked before recording; transcribed by an AI tool. The audio is deleted once these notes are merged. Interviewed by Joseph Etse and Liza Aikins.
**Removed from these notes:** her name and her contact details (kept privately by the team for follow-up only).

## How the facility works

- **Volume:** "On average… 15 to 20" patients a day, and some days none. Patients don't wait. This is far below an urban hospital OPD.
- **Busy days and seasons:** Mondays and Tuesdays. November and December are quiet because people are preparing for Christmas. January has a surge of people who say they have been ill since December. Many try herbal remedies or a pharmacy first.
- **Flow:** vitals with the nurse first, then the prescriber. Anyone not improving within 24 hours is referred.
- **Staffing:** one Physician Assistant who lives on site, available unless he is on leave or off duty. Nurses also prescribe for minor ailments, which takes load off him. Some patients prefer to see the nurse.
- **The nurse does everything:** "you are going for the folder, you are checking vitals, you'll be consulting, still you'll be prescribing… dispensing the medicine, everything."
- **Records:** paper folders, found through the patient's hospital card. Without the card, a folder can only be found if the visit was recent, using the consulting book. Older folders take too long to search.
- **Computers:** one doesn't work. The other is used only for NHIS insurance claims. Everything else is manual.
- **Contact:** patients in the community come to the nurse's house rather than calling.
- **Absent prescriber:** when the PA is on leave, the nurses know but "you can't tell the community". Patients come, find him absent and are disappointed.
- **What would make work easier:** "Someone who dispenses the medicine, then a front desk, then someone who goes for the folder."
