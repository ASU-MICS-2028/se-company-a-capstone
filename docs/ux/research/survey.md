# Survey draft: waiting at the OPD (#29)

**Status: draft for Liza to edit before it goes out.** Build it in Google Forms, with responses not linked to email addresses. Target: anyone who has visited, or gone with someone to, a public hospital or polyclinic outpatient department (OPD) in Ghana in the last 12 months. Aim for 20+ responses. It takes about 3 minutes.

## Opening text (shown before the questions)

> We are students at Ashesi University designing a system to cut waiting time at hospital outpatient departments. This survey takes about 3 minutes. It is anonymous: we do not ask your name, your phone number or anything about your health. You can stop at any time. Answers are used only for our class project and research paper.
>
> ☐ I agree to take part.

## Screening

0. In the last 12 months, have you visited a public hospital or polyclinic OPD in Ghana, for yourself or with someone else?
   *Yes / No (form ends)*

## Questions

1. **On your most recent visit, what time did you arrive?**
   *Before 6 a.m. / 6–8 a.m. / 8–10 a.m. / After 10 a.m. / I don't remember*

2. **About how long did you wait in total, from arriving to seeing the doctor?**
   *Under 1 hour / 1–2 hours / 2–4 hours / More than 4 hours*

3. **What did the visit cost you, apart from fees? (choose all that apply)**
   *Lost work or income / Missed school or lectures / Closed my shop or stall / Paid for childcare / Two trips in one day / Nothing / Other: ___*

4. **Which phone do you use most?**
   *Button phone (no apps) / Smartphone / I share a phone / No phone*

5. **If a text message told you when to come so you didn't have to wait at the clinic, how likely would you be to use it?**
   *Very likely / Likely / Not sure / Unlikely / Very unlikely*

6. **(Open)** What would make waiting at the OPD easier for you?

## Optional, last page

- Age group: *Under 25 / 25–39 / 40–59 / 60 and above / Prefer not to say*
- Language you'd prefer for messages: *English / Twi / Ga / Ewe / Other: ___*

## Why each question is there

| Q | Tests | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 | Is the dawn-arrival, long-wait pattern from the studies true for our respondents? | Problem statement, data generator calibration (#9) |
| 3 | What the wait costs, which no study we found measured | Problem statement, paper |
| 4 | How many need USSD/SMS rather than an app | Channel priority (#5) |
| 5 | Would people trust a "come now" text? | Main risk to the concept |
| 6 | Needs we haven't thought of | Requirements (#5) |
| Language | Twi, Ga or Ewe templates | UX (#6) |
