How it works

Three steps, and no early-morning wait

Join from any phone

Dial the Twɛn code, send an SMS, use the website, or ask at the front desk. You get a ticket number straight away.

Wait wherever you are

Twɛn tells you about how long you will wait. Open your stall or go to work, and check your place at any time.

Come when we text you

One “come now” text arrives in time for your journey. Twɛn learns how long you usually take to get there.

Check my wait

Your place in the queue, any time

Enter the ticket number from your SMS to see where you are and when to leave. You can also dial the code and choose option 2.

For clinic staff

A queue board that keeps working when the network drops

The board runs in the browser on any clinic computer or tablet. If the internet goes down it keeps working, then catches up when the connection returns. No “come now” text is sent twice or lost.

  • Call next, mark no-showsOne tap, and the patient's text goes out.
  • Priority stays with staffStaff decide who goes first: pregnant women, older patients, emergencies. The system never decides this.
  • Desk ticketsPatients without a phone get a printed number at the desk.

How we earn your trust

Honest about waits and careful with your data

We test our estimates

A model predicts each wait. We compare it with two simpler methods, a standard queueing formula and a fixed rule based on the time of day, and keep the model only if it is clearly more accurate.

We collect very little

Twɛn needs only your phone number and your ticket. It never asks what is wrong with you. We follow Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

We built it for real conditions

The service is designed for button phones, weak signal and power cuts. A text that fails to send is retried until it is delivered, and it is never sent twice.
