# The instructor is Product Owner; Liza owns requirements and UX

## Context

[Roles and workstream owners](2026-11-18-roles-and-workstream-owners.md) made Liza Aikins Product Owner. That was a mistake. In Lecture 5 the lecturer says he is the Product Owner for the class, because he is the one who represents the class. So the Product Owner role is already filled, and it sits outside our team.

## Decision

**Scrum roles:** the lecturer, Dr. Adjepon-Yamoah, is Product Owner. Joseph Etse is Scrum Master. All six team members are developers.

**Liza Aikins** keeps #5 Requirements and #6 UX and wireframes, as **Requirements and UX lead**. She writes the user stories and acceptance criteria and keeps the backlog ready for the Product Owner to order. She has built several mobile app interfaces.

Every other owner in the earlier decision stays the same.

## Options we rejected

- **Keep a student Product Owner as well.** Scrum has one Product Owner, not a committee. A second one would split who decides the order of the backlog.

## Consequences

- Backlog order, scope changes and sprint acceptance go to the lecturer. We bring them to class or to office hours.
- Liza prepares the stories and acceptance criteria so his decisions are quick.
- The README's team table changes in its own PR, because it is a root file.
- The lanyard cards say "Requirements and UX" for Liza.
