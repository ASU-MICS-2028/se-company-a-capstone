# Name the company Nyansapo Systems

## Context

The capstone runs each group as a company. We needed a name and logo that say something true about us, work for any product we pick, and suit an ACM conference audience.

## Decision

**Nyansapo Systems.** Nyansapo, the "wisdom knot", is the Adinkra symbol for wisdom, ingenuity, intelligence and patience. It fits an MSc in Intelligent Computing Systems and the brief's requirement for an intelligent system. Team vote on 13 Nov 2026: Nyansapo 5, Nkonsonson 3, Hexweave 3, SixNines 1.

## Options we rejected

- **Nkonsonson Labs** (chain links, unity): strong meaning, but hard to spell on first hearing.
- **Hexweave** (kente, six threads): plain English, but the mark loses detail at small sizes.
- **SixNines** (99.9999% availability): fits the course's dependability theme, but sets a bar the MVP would have to defend.
- **Akofena:** the instructor's own site brand, so it would read as flattery.

## Consequences

- The logo on the concept board is a sketch. #4 redraws it from the authentic glyph.
- Palette: Ashesi maroon `#8a1c2b`, kente gold `#e0a21b`, green `#1e6a3c`.
- A screen reader may mispronounce "Nyansapo". Say it as "n-YAN-sa-poh" in talks, and keep "Systems" in the full name.
