# Handling feedback mid-sprint

Scenario from Quiz 1: halfway through a Sprint, users report that unlocking a bike is confusing and unsafe. What should the team do?

The marking scheme gives one mark each for:
1. **Recognising** the safety and usability report as important feedback.
2. **Investigating** it with users and evidence before acting.
3. **Collaborating** as a team (with the PO) on the response.
4. **Keeping Sprint focus**, without uncontrolled scope growth.
5. **Using the findings to adapt** later backlog priorities and product decisions.

"Just add the new requirement to the Sprint" does not get full marks.

The Scrum Guide's rule behind this: during a Sprint, make no changes that endanger the Sprint Goal, but scope *may be clarified and renegotiated with the PO*. If the fix serves the goal (here, safe unlocking *is* the goal), swap it in for lower-value items. If the goal itself becomes obsolete, only the PO may cancel the Sprint.

## Use it in the capstone

When the instructor's 10 Dec checkpoint feedback arrives mid-sprint, log it as backlog items. Discuss it in the next stand-up, and record the decision (swap in, defer, or re-plan) in the sprint notes. That record is good Scrum evidence.
