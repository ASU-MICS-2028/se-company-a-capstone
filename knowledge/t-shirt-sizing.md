# T-shirt sizing

**T-shirt sizing** is a rough way to estimate the scope and effort of a task or project with labels such as XS, S, M, L, XL instead of hours or points. For example, a one-to-two-week task is "S", and something taking a month or two is "L" or "XL".

Why use it: early on (in the Define stage of the Double Diamond, or first backlog grooming), precise estimates are fake precision. Relative sizes are quick, easy for non-developers to join in, and good enough to decide what fits a deadline. Later, convert to story points for sprint planning.

## Use it in the capstone

T-shirt-size every candidate feature at proposal time. With 6 weeks, anything XL either gets split or goes to "future work" in the feasibility scope.
