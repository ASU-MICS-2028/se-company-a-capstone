# Generate diagrams with AI and PlantUML

**PlantUML** is a tool that turns plain-text descriptions into UML diagrams (use case, sequence, class and more). The lecturer explicitly allowed AI for diagramming:

1. Write or refine your requirements and scenarios.
2. Ask an AI assistant (ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Claude, etc.) to turn them into PlantUML text, saved for example as a Markdown or text file.
3. Feed that into PlantUML to get the diagram immediately.

He also mentioned connecting Figma to AI assistants to speed up interface work. The point of the course is not hand-drawing or hand-coding websites; use tools so you can focus on the engineering thinking. Disclose AI use as required by the course's AI disclosure form.

## Use it in the capstone

Keep PlantUML (or similar diagrams-as-code) source files for our use case and sequence diagrams in the repo, so they can be versioned and regenerated when requirements change.
