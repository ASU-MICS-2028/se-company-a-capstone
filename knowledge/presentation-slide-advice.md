# Presentation slide advice

From the lecturer's feedback on team presentations:

- **Use the university slide templates.** There are about six; pick one.
- **Font sizes must be readable.** Use the space on the slide instead of squeezing text into a corner.
- **Show a picture of each persona** so the audience can relate to the user.
- Use an illustration library (he mentioned free illustration sets) to make slides look serious.
- **Colour schemes must be intentional.** If building for an institution, use its established colours; any other choice must be deliberate.
- Put key evidence (for example, the requirement classification table) on the slides, not only in the report, because he marks what he sees.
- Disclose AI use: there is an **AI disclosure form**. You may use AI tools, but say where.

## Use it in the capstone

Apply these to our conference talk and any sprint demo slides, and fill in the AI disclosure form for the paper and code.
