# Multilingual design for Africa

A hidden assumption in most designs is that everyone reads English. The lecturer gave two examples:

- His university hosted French-speaking students relocated from a university in Niger. Many had to learn English first, and still prefer French. A written menu or app in English only excludes them.
- On the learning-management system his team built, captions are available in several languages chosen by research to cover Africa: **English**, **Hausa** (widely spoken across West Africa), **Swahili** (East Africa, Kenya to Rwanda and Uganda), **Portuguese**, and **Arabic** for North Africa (the model was still weak). A sign-language avatar uses Ghanaian Sign Language, with plans for other African sign languages.

If you build for Africa, include languages representative of the regions you serve, and let users choose (for example, a language selector in the app, or augmented-reality explanations).

## Use it in the capstone

Our system targets an African problem, so the requirements doc should state which languages we support and why, and the paper should justify the choice with evidence.
