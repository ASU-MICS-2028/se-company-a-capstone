# Capstone research question

**Start by posing a research question.** Every research paper needs one, and it should drive the work. The capstone is not just "an applied project where you build a system and you're happy". It needs a research component.

- The system you build is the **proof of concept** that answers the question (for example, that your model or approach works in a particular way).
- The question can be about the AI component (how well a model performs in this setting), security (how to secure end-to-end engagement), reliability, and so on.
- You may have 2 or 3 questions, but **one is enough**.

**Using one project across courses** (for example, NLP coursework or a thesis) is fine. The research question can belong to the other course, as long as you build a software prototype that proves it, with the full software engineering around it.

The paper is presented at the internal Ashesi software engineering research conference. The lecturer gives feedback, so that by the end of the course it is mature enough to submit to a real conference.

## Use it in the capstone

Write the research question into the proposal first. Then make sure the architecture, the test plan and the evaluation produce the data the paper needs to answer it.
