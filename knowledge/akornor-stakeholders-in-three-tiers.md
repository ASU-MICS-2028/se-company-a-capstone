# Akornor stakeholders in three tiers

A **stakeholder** is anyone affected by, or able to affect, the system. We sorted Akornor's into three tiers by how close they are to the ordering screens.

**Primary: they order and collect.**
- Students with visual impairments (the group the design centres on)
- Students with other access needs (ADHD, dyslexia, motor, hearing)
- Students without impairments, and faculty and staff (fast, accurate orders in a short break)

**Secondary: they work around the system.**
- Servers and cashiers (hand over the right order without extra work)
- Kitchen staff (clear item and customisation data)
- Delivery dispatchers (clear location, confirm the right person got it)
- Akornor management (throughput, revenue reconciliation)
- Cafeteria Management Committee (commissioned the work)

**Tertiary: they shape it from outside.**
- Disability and student support services (accessibility commitments)
- IT department (student records, meal plan, sign-in, tablet upkeep)
- Payment providers, Mobile Money and card (their PIN and OTP steps are limits we cannot change)
- University leadership
- Guests and visitors, deliberately excluded (constraint CON07: no student ID or meal plan)

Writing "interest in the system" next to each group exposed requirements we would otherwise miss, such as the staff-side rule that a meal cannot be published without allergen data (NFR07).

Start the capstone stakeholder section from this table; add the tier and one-line interest for any new group.
