# Quiz and exam expectations

What the lecturer said about assessment in Lectures 4 and 5:

- **Cover everything.** Every aspect of a software engineering project matters, including what is implied but not said. Review the videos and links he shares.
- **Exam hints** (he said he rarely gives them): the **double diamond** method will appear; and detailed, named **personas** with specific needs matter.
- **Quiz format**: on Canvas, based on the textbook theory. One part is scenario-based and practical (apply theory to a scenario); the other has two or three theory questions asking you to explain a concept. No objective (multiple-choice) questions.
- **Readings** for the Scrum week: textbook chapter 2, an assigned research paper, and the **Scrum Guide** by Schwaber and Sutherland (studied in depth in a later agile course). His slide summaries motivate reading; they don't replace it.
- The course assumes prior software engineering experience, in class or practice. Anyone without it should ask him for a side plan.
- **Reimagining task submission**: one PDF, sectioned into the in-person layout (ideally one picture that brings everything together; at most two unless you can justify more perspectives) and the app view. Each team reimagines for its own focal disability.
- Work at a high level of excellence and be thorough.

## Use it in the capstone

Before each deliverable, check it covers both the explicit rubric and the implied expectations (double diamond, personas, Scrum theory), and cite the readings in the ACM paper.
