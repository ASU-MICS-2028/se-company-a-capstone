# Kofi Mensah, our primary persona

A **persona** is a made-up but realistic user we design for, so arguments become "would Kofi manage this?" instead of opinion. Kofi is fictional.

**Who he is.** 24, MSc student, totally blind since age nine. Android phone with TalkBack (the Android screen reader, which speaks what is under your finger) always on. Earphones in noisy places. Types slowly, so prefers picking from lists. Pays with his campus meal plan. Has a nut (peanut) allergy.

**Context.** Ten to fifteen minutes between classes. Akornor at 12:30 is crowded and loud, so phone speech competes with the noise.

**Goals.** Order and collect without asking anyone; know the price before paying; avoid nuts; confirm the dish he collects is the one he paid for; know when it is ready.

**Pain points today.** Wall menu board he must ask a stranger to read; receipts he cannot check; plates he cannot verify; no signal that his turn has come.

**Quote.** "I don't mind waiting. I mind not knowing what I'm eating."

**Secondary persona: Ama**, 24, low vision from glaucoma. Uses 200% text, high contrast, large tap targets, and needs status as text, not colour.

Every requirement, screen and model we produced traces back to one of Kofi's goals or pain points.

## Use it in the capstone

Reuse Kofi (and Ama) as the test user for any new feature: write the scenario from his side first, then check each screen against his goals.
