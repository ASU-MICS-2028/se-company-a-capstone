# Journal entries follow the daily scrum questions

The lecturer said the two-weekly **journal** should take the form of a **daily scrum** (the short Scrum stand-up meeting). Answer three critical questions:

1. **What was done?** What you learned on your own, what you learned in class, and what you read.
2. **What will you do?** Your plan going forward.
3. **What is blocking you?** The bottlenecks and challenges, and the measures you think would help you do better.

Other logistics from the same session:
- Teams were asked to submit two reimagined designs: the cafeteria **experience** for visually impaired and ADHD users, and one **interface** (mobile app or website; it may focus on one part, such as payment, the landing page, or ordering). Outside reviewers were to be invited to help judge.
- Teams should update and **prioritise** their requirements after the system-diagram session.
- A class field trip was planned, preferably something IT-related.

## Use it in the capstone

Use the same done / next / blockers structure for our Scrum records and sprint journals, so they double as course journal entries.
