# Quiz 1 Bamboo Bike rubric

**Scenario:** a campus Bamboo Bike system (locate, unlock, track, report faults, return; GPS, e-lock, notifications, admin dashboard). Users vary: occasional and regular riders, people with limited digital literacy, visual or motor impairments, or unreliable mobile data. The stated principle is that **technology should follow users' needs**, not be added because it is possible.

**Structure:** 60 marks in 80 minutes. Part A, UX, Q1–Q5 at 10 marks each. Part B, Scrum, Q6 at 10 marks. Bonus Q7 at 10 marks.

| Q | Topic | What earns marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Users and stakeholders | 4 groups, 4 goals, 4 pain points, link needs to design, danger of a single "typical" user |
| 2 | User research | Methods with purpose; what each reveals; fit to context; stated vs observed vs contextual behaviour |
| 3 | Personas and scenarios | Two evidence-based personas, accessibility, scenarios, how personas reduce assumptions |
| 4 | Evaluation | 4 UX problems (QR-code dependence, connectivity, unsafe use while riding, battery), 3 methods, right participants, inclusion |
| 5 | Double Diamond | Concrete activities per stage, diverge vs converge, the cost of fixing on a solution too early |
| 6 | Scrum basics | Backlogs, timeboxed Sprint, team and Daily Scrum, Increment, feedback and adaptation |
| 7 | Applying Scrum | First-sprint picks (locate, unlock, track, report damage), an outcome-style Sprint Goal, handling mid-sprint safety feedback |

## Use it in the capstone

Reuse the Q4 problem list as a checklist for our own UX risks: offline use, non-visual feedback, battery and safety. Reuse Q7's pattern for our first Sprint Goal: one coherent user outcome, not a task list.
