# Mid-semester interview and exam

Logistics the lecturer gave in Lectures 8 and 9:

- The mid-semester assessment includes an **individual interview** alongside the exam.
- **Format:** each student gets roughly ten to fifteen minutes. You get a few minutes and some paper to think and jot down points, then you explain your answer to the lecturer. It is **not a panel**. It is **recorded on Zoom** in case anyone disputes the outcome later.
- In Lecture 9 it was planned for the following Monday (a three-hour slot shared among the students).
- The **quiz** that was due was moved to after the mid-semester break.
- He said he wants to keep the class light, so people don't feel overwhelmed, while still being rigorous and making room for remote participants.

Topics he flagged as likely interview questions:

- The difference between the **Definition of Done** and **acceptance criteria**.
- **Estimation** approaches beyond Fibonacci story points.
- How a **Scrum Master** differs from a **project manager** (set as a research task).

## Use it in the capstone

Each team member should be able to explain any of our Scrum artefacts and diagrams on their own. The interview is individual, so don't let one person own all the knowledge.
