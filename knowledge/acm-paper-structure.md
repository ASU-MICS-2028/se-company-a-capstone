# ACM paper structure for the capstone

**Length:** 6–10 pages, **ACM conference template**. Peer-reviewed at ASERC. Start writing on 14 Nov, and look for a relevant 2027 ACM conference to aim at.

Required sections, in the brief's order:
1. Abstract
2. Keywords
3. Introduction and problem motivation
4. Background and related work
5. System design and architecture
6. Methodology: the UX, Agile and programming model choices
7. Evaluation: performance tests and quality metrics
8. Results
9. Discussion and limitations
10. Ethical analysis / threats to research validity
11. Conclusion and future work
12. References, in ACM style

Also required: an **AI Use Declaration**. The brief allows responsible AI use if it is documented in the paper.

**Threats to validity** means the reasons our results might be wrong or might not generalise: small samples, self-reported data, a test environment unlike real use. The readings model this well. Ghimire and Charters say plainly that their survey shows correlation, not causation.

The rubric gives the paper 5% under Documentation ("insightful, well-referenced"). The brief's summary table gives it 20%. See [Capstone grade weights](capstone-grade-weights.md).

## Use it in the capstone

Create the paper skeleton with these headings on day one. Write Methodology and System design during the sprints, while decisions are fresh. Evaluation needs real numbers from 11–14 Dec.
