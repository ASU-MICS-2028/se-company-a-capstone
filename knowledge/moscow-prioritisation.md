# MoSCoW prioritisation

**MoSCoW** is a way to rank requirements by how essential they are:

- **Must have** - the system cannot work or be accepted without it.
- **Should have** - important and expected, but the system can still function without it for now.
- **Could have** - nice to have; flexible, drop it if time or money runs out.
- (Many texts add **Won't have (this time)** for things explicitly left out.)

The lecturer's example: "A user must register on the app" - is that a must or a should? Deciding forces the team to think about what is truly essential. He also argued elsewhere in the lecture that forcing registration before browsing can lose customers, so the priority is a design decision, not just a label.

Use MoSCoW together with requirement IDs when you formalise the requirements.

## Use it in the capstone

Tag every requirement in our requirements doc with M/S/C (and W). Use the Musts to define our minimum viable product and the first sprints' backlog.
