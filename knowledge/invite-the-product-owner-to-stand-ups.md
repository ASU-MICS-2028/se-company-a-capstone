# Invite the Product Owner to stand-ups

The project description does not list stand-ups. But teams run the project with Scrum, so the lecturer expects them to hold daily stand-ups anyway. He asked each team to invite him to its stand-ups the following week. He is the Product Owner for the class, the person who orders the backlog and speaks for the client.

He also proposed cancelling one class session the following week, so that teams could focus on their projects.

## Use it in the capstone

Send the lecturer the stand-up time and link for the week of 24 Nov, and keep each stand-up to the three daily-scrum questions. Log each one in the Scrum records (#15). Hold impediments that need his decision for the end of the stand-up, while he is there.
