# Akornor Scrum teaching deck summary

**Argument.** Scrum shown end to end on a familiar campus problem: long queues, unclear menus and poor accessibility at Akornor cafeteria.

**Walk-through:**
- **Persona:** a second-year student who needs a fast, accessible way to browse, order and skip the queue.
- **Product Goal:** faster, clearer, more accessible ordering. Cut waiting, end confusion at the counter, and let students with disabilities order independently.
- **Product Vision:** any student can browse, order and track from their phone, anytime, without barriers.
- **Sprint Goal:** students can browse the menu and order digitally, cutting queue time by 50%. Note that it is *measurable*.
- **Sprint Backlog:** view menu by category; add to cart and confirm; confirmation notification; admin marks order ready; basic screen-reader labels.
- **Daily Scrum:** 15 minutes. Name the blocker, give it an owner, adapt the plan.
- **Sprint Review:** demo the working increment to students and staff and turn their feedback into backlog items. The demo increment meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and focus rules, with keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels and audio and visual feedback.
- **Retrospective techniques:** see [Sprint retrospective](sprint-retrospective.md). Actions get owners and deadlines and are tracked on Trello.
- **Backlog refinement and Definition of Done:** refine and estimate stories, and meet the acceptance criteria.

## Use it in the capstone

Copy the shape: product goal, then a measurable sprint goal, then 4–6 backlog items including one accessibility item in *every* sprint, then a demo to real users.
