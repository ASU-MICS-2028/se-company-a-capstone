# Burndown chart

A **burndown chart** shows **how much work is left** in a sprint.

- **Y-axis:** remaining work (story points or tasks). **X-axis:** time (sprint days).
- An **ideal line** runs straight from the total at day 0 to zero on the last day.
- The **actual line** is updated daily as items reach Done.

Reading it: actual *above* ideal means behind; *below* means ahead. A **flat stretch** means nothing was finished on those days, often a sign of a blocker or of items too big to finish.

Weakness: if scope is added mid-sprint, the line simply looks slow. A [burnup chart](burnup-chart.md) shows that scope growth separately.

Example from my notes: a 10-day sprint of 40 points, plotted D0–D10.

## Use it in the capstone

The brief explicitly asks for burndown charts. Record remaining points every day (GitHub Projects insights, Jira, or a spreadsheet) and export a chart per sprint into `docs/agile/`.
