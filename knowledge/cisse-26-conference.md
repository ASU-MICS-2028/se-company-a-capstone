# CISSE 26 conference (the ASERC venue)

The poster shared with the rubric announces the **Conference for Intelligent and Secured Software Engineering 2026 (CISSE 26)**.

- **Theme:** Intelligent Computing Systems for an Inclusive Africa
- **Tagline:** Innovate, Research, Impact
- **Dates:** 18–19 December 2026
- **Venue:** Ashesi University, Berekuso campus
- **Audience:** students and faculty, researchers and innovators, industry partners, policy makers and tech enthusiasts
- **Highlights:** capstone projects, research presentations, expert panels, networking
- **Info:** ashesi.edu.gh/cisse

The brief calls our venue the "Ashesi Software Engineering Research Conference (ASERC)" on 18 Dec. The rubric is headed "ASERC Ready!". CISSE 26 appears to be the public name of the same event; confirm with the instructor.

The theme words matter: **intelligent**, **secured** and **inclusive**. They match rubric sections A (intelligent components), D (scalable, *secure* design) and F (ethics and inclusion, African relevance).

## Use it in the capstone

Frame the paper title and talk around the theme: an intelligent, secure system that widens inclusion in an African setting. Expect an outside audience (industry, policy makers), so explain impact in plain terms.
