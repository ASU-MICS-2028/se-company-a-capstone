# System Usability Scale (SUS)

The **SUS** is a standard 10-question questionnaire, answered on a 1–5 agree-to-disagree scale after using a system. Harte et al. list it among the metrics for Phase 3 user testing.

Scoring:
- Odd-numbered items (positively worded): score = answer − 1.
- Even-numbered items (negatively worded): score = 5 − answer.
- Sum the ten scores and multiply by **2.5**, giving 0–100.

A score of about **68 is average**. Above 80 is good. Below 50 signals serious problems. It is not a percentage.

Why use it: it is free, quick, works with small samples (5–10 users give a usable signal), and is widely cited, so a reviewer can compare our score with others.

## Use it in the capstone

Give the SUS to every test user in sprint review. Report the mean and range in the paper's Results, and state the sample size under threats to validity.
