# Scenarios group requirements

A **scenario** is a sub-group of requirements that, on its own, can be implemented, makes sense, and delivers a service.

The lecturer borrowed from theatre: a play has chapters, and each chapter has scenes. A scene is a series of interactions on stage; when it ends, everyone exits and the next scene begins. A scene is not the whole story, just one meaningful part of it.

How to use scenarios:
1. Summarise and break down the full requirements list.
2. Group related requirements into scenarios (for example, "customer orders food on the drone app").
3. Create designs (diagrams) for each scenario.
4. Implement one scenario at a time, starting from its diagram.
5. Depending on the methodology, iterate on it or move to the next scenario.

## Use it in the capstone

Scenarios map naturally onto Scrum epics or sprint goals. Group our requirement IDs into scenarios and use one or two scenarios per sprint.
