# Capstone milestones and dates

Project period: **09 Nov – 18 Dec 2026** (the brief says 6 weeks, but groups them into three "weeks").

**Week 1 (09–14 Nov): planning, research and design**
| Date | Activity or deliverable |
|---|---|
| 09–11 Nov | Team formation ("Company"), topic brainstorming and vote |
| 13 Nov | Submit half-page Project Idea / Concept Note; fast instructor feedback |
| **14 Nov** | **Milestone 1: full project proposal** |

**Week 2 (14 Nov – 10 Dec): implementation and documentation**
| Date | Activity or deliverable |
|---|---|
| 14 Nov | Start writing the ACM paper; look for relevant 2027 ACM conference events |
| 14–16 Nov | System architecture, UX wireframes (Figma), data design |
| 16 Nov – 10 Dec | Implement core features |
| **10 Dec** | **Milestone 2: architecture + UX + core system demo checkpoint** |

**Week 3 (11–18 Dec): refinement, testing, paper, presentation**
| Date | Activity or deliverable |
|---|---|
| 11–14 Dec | Testing, performance measures, reliability calculations |
| 14–16 Dec | Finalise ACM paper and documentation |
| 17 Dec | Internal practice presentations |
| **18 Dec** | **Final submission and ASERC conference presentations** |

Submissions go on **Canvas**, which has slots for both milestones. Late work loses 10% of the project grade per day unless formally excused. The lecturer called the work after 10 Dec "just paperwork" and warned: stick to the schedule and don't leave the paper to the last minute.

Other course dates from the syllabus: Quiz 5 on 19 Nov; a lab or research discussion due 21 Nov. The CISSE 26 poster lists the conference as 18–19 Dec 2026 at the Berekuso campus.

## Use it in the capstone

Plan sprints backwards from 10 Dec and 18 Dec: sprint 1 covers proposal and diagrams, the middle sprints implementation, the last one hardening, tests, paper and talk. Milestone 2 needs working code, not slides. Leave 11–14 Dec for measurement, because the paper's Evaluation and Results depend on it.
