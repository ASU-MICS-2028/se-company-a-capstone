# Act like a real company

The lecturer said the two class companies had not yet worked as professionally as he wanted. His expectations:

- **Identity:** each company has its own name, **logo, tagline, vision and mission**, developed by the team. Wear a consistent look: team lanyards to every class, especially Monday sessions, and name the company and display it in class.
- **Work like industry:** assign tasks from the start, set times, and run **rigorous daily scrums**, and actually meet their goals.
- **Specialise:** each member becomes the expert in their assigned area, but everyone shares knowledge.
- **Meeting conduct:** he may attend meetings to see whether everyone gets heard, whether the chair lets people share their thoughts fully, and whether good HR practices are followed.
- **Clients don't care about your midterms.** He floated paid client work through the venture incubator (see the opportunities note), which needs real deadline discipline.

## Use it in the capstone

Nyansapo Systems needs a logo, tagline, vision and mission on the repo README and the slides. Daily scrum notes and task assignments go in the Scrum records.
