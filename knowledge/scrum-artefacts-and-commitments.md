# Scrum artefacts and their commitments

Each Scrum artefact is paired with a **commitment**, the target that makes progress measurable:

| Artefact | What it is | Commitment |
|---|---|---|
| **Product Backlog** | Emergent, ordered list of everything needed to improve the product; the single source of work | **Product Goal**: the long-term future state of the product |
| **Sprint Backlog** | Sprint Goal (why) + selected items (what) + actionable plan (how); owned by Developers, updated daily | **Sprint Goal**: the single objective of this Sprint |
| **Increment** | A usable, verified step toward the Product Goal; adds to all earlier increments | **Definition of Done**: the quality bar; work that misses it returns to the backlog |

Scrum's five values: commitment, focus, openness, respect and courage.

The Guide's point: Scrum exists only in its entirety. Using parts of it is possible, but the result is not Scrum. This matches Ghimire and Charters' "Agile-lite" warning.

## Use it in the capstone

Write our Product Goal at the top of the board, a Sprint Goal on each sprint, and the Definition of Done in the repo's CONTRIBUTING or README. All three are evidence for rubric C.
