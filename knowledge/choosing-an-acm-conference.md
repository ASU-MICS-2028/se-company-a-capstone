# Choosing an ACM conference

**ACM** is the Association for Computing Machinery. In computer science and software engineering, researchers mostly target ACM and **IEEE** conferences. The lecturer encourages joining at least one of them. Membership gives you papers, recorded talks, and discounts on conference fees.

**How to use the ACM conference list he shared (2027 conferences):**

1. Browse the calendar of ACM conferences by month.
2. Pick one whose theme connects to our research question.
3. Open its **previous editions** and read the papers published there. Ashesi's subscription gives full access when you are logged in on campus or through the university.
4. Pick a few papers close to our topic and **model our paper on them**: structure, length and evaluation style.
5. Follow that conference's **author guidelines** (page limits, format, expected outcomes).

The course paper is 6 to 10 pages in ACM format.

## Use it in the capstone

Choose the target venue early (sprint 1). Save 3 to 5 model papers in the repo, and shape the paper's sections and evaluation around that venue's norms.
