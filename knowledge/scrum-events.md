# Scrum events and timeboxes

The **Sprint** contains all other events. It is a fixed length of one month or less, and the next starts as soon as one ends. During a Sprint: nothing that endangers the Sprint Goal, no drop in quality, and the backlog is refined as needed. Only the PO can cancel a Sprint, and only if its goal becomes obsolete.

| Event | Purpose | Max timebox (1-month sprint) |
|---|---|---|
| Sprint Planning | Why (Sprint Goal), what (items), how (plan) | 8 h |
| Daily Scrum | Developers inspect progress toward the goal and adapt the plan | 15 min |
| Sprint Review | Inspect the Increment with stakeholders; adapt the backlog. A working session, not a slideshow | 4 h |
| Sprint Retrospective | Improve quality and effectiveness; ends the Sprint | 3 h |

Shorter sprints get proportionally shorter events. **Backlog refinement** is ongoing, not a formal event: split, describe, order and size items.

## Use it in the capstone

With about 5 weeks of build time, run 1-week sprints, with planning under 1 h, review and retro of about 45 min each, and stand-ups (even async in chat) every working day. Save notes from every review and retro for the documentation package.
