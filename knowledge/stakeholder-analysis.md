# Stakeholder analysis

A **stakeholder** is anyone who affects or is affected by the project. Those *affected* have an interest in it. Those who *can affect* it have power over it. Not all stakeholders are direct users: in the Bamboo Bike quiz, campus security, maintenance staff, the Sustainability Office and visitors all count.

Steps:
1. Identify all stakeholders.
2. Rank them by importance to the project's success (for example, on a power vs interest grid).
3. Understand each one's requirements, goals and pain points.
4. Communicate with them and get them involved.
5. Manage their expectations.

The grader wants each stakeholder's needs *connected to design decisions*, and an explanation of why designing for one "typical" user is dangerous.

## Use it in the capstone

The proposal needs a "Stakeholder analysis" section. Make a table: stakeholder | direct or indirect | power/interest | goal | pain point | design decision it drives.
