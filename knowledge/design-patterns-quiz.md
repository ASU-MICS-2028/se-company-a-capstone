# Design patterns quiz

What was said about the patterns quiz:

- It covers **all 23** Gang of Four patterns. It was moved several times (Lecture 11 to Monday, Lecture 12 to Thursday, then a practice test promised in Lecture 13 for the following Monday).
- The format is **riddle-style**. You name the pattern and give a short explanation. The lecturer said it would be "very simplified".
- He promised **five practice riddles** to discuss in the class WhatsApp group.
- Lecture 13 described elimination-style practice rounds and questions on the architectural and practical use of the patterns.
- **Know the Gang of Four authors' surnames** (Gamma, Helm, Johnson, Vlissides). "Facts are important." He doesn't want rote memorisation as the basis of learning, but key facts must be known.
- Expected question: *why is Singleton overused, and why does that harm design?*
- Know **why** each pattern is in its family, and how similar patterns differ (Strategy vs State, Adapter vs Decorator vs Proxy, Flyweight vs Prototype, Singleton vs Facade, Abstract Factory vs Factory Method).

## Use it in the capstone

The same distinctions let us justify our pattern choices in the architecture docs and in the Q&A at the final presentation.
