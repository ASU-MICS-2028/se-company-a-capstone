# Midsem interview Q1 rubric (understanding the users)

The interview question had three parts, all built on the Akornor cafeteria personas: a visually impaired student and a student with ADHD.

1. **UX:** three challenges for each persona with a conventional ordering app.
   - Visual: images without alt text, poor keyboard navigation, missing screen-reader labels, low contrast, inaccessible dynamic menus, unlabelled form fields.
   - ADHD: information overload, too many choices, distracting animation, unclear navigation, long checkout, inconsistent layouts, badly timed notifications.
2. **Requirements:** turn two challenges per persona into concise "The system shall…" statements. For example: "provide meaningful alternative text for meal images"; "allow complete keyboard navigation"; "present checkout as a short, clearly sequenced process".
3. **Scrum:** the Product Owner captures needs as backlog items or user stories. The team agrees acceptance criteria. Items are prioritised and refined before Sprint Planning, and finished items join the Increment.

Marks out of 10: visual UX 2, ADHD UX 2, four requirements 3, Scrum explanation 2, clarity 1.

## Use it in the capstone

This is the chain the individual interview will probe: user barrier → "shall" requirement → backlog item with acceptance criteria → Increment. Each of us should be able to walk one of our own features through it.
