# Opportunities: grants, client work and memberships

Opportunities and resources the lecturer mentioned:

- **AWS research grant:** up to US$50,000 for one year, on **African languages**. The deadline was 11 November 2026 (now passed), but the call may come round again.
- **Paid client work:** the lecturer has outside clients with needs. Through the Ashesi Venture Incubator (founders who are often non-technical) and the entrepreneurship centre, companies could take on real paid projects, likely starting small during the Christmas break. He warned that clients don't care about your exam schedule. Undergraduate teams have done this before.
- **ACM and IEEE membership:** access to papers and talks, plus conference discounts. He has renewed his every year since around 2018.
- **Textbook:** Sommerville's *Software Engineering* is his go-to. It is expensive but thorough. Using AI to summarise it is fine, but don't lose the essentials.
- **Interviews:** a large financial-data firm recruits at Ashesi roughly yearly, mostly final-year undergraduates. Expect AI-assisted system design questions.
- **Guests:** a UK-based software engineer and a visiting researcher from Portugal were lined up to speak.

## Use it in the capstone

If our system has a local-language angle, cite the AWS call as evidence of funder interest. A stakeholder from the incubator could serve as a real client.
