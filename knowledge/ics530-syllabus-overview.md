# ICS 530 syllabus overview

MSc/MPhil Intelligent Computing Systems, Semester 1, 4 credits. The course uses **Problem-Based Learning (PBL)**: weekly problem situations ("prosits") that span requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment and maintenance. Ethics is built into every cycle.

**Topics:** SE overview; UX design (systems); Agile (Scrum, XP, Kanban); software architectures and programming models; software quality engineering; applied data programming; ethical practice.

**Learning objectives (abridged):** evaluate user needs and apply HCI and usability heuristics; analyse how Agile affects quality, productivity and stakeholder satisfaction; compare layered, microservice and event-driven styles; implement with suitable programming models (OO, functional, reactive); apply unit, integration, system and acceptance testing; use automated testing and CI; write quality plans aligned to ISO and IEEE.

**Grading:** assignments 20% (journaling 10%, research discussions 10%); quizzes 20%; mid-semester exam 20%; final 40% (team project and research 30%, individual interview 10%).

**Textbooks:** Sommerville, *Engineering Software Products* (2021), "ESP"; Beare and Allanwood, *User Experience Design* (2019). Secondary: Sommerville's *Software Engineering*, Ghezzi et al., Brooks, DeMarco and Lister.

**Assigned papers:** Harte et al. 2017 (HCD); Ghimire and Charters 2022 (Agile); Hasselbring 2018 (architecture); Colakoglu et al. 2021 (quality metrics); Belcastro et al. 2022 (big data programming); Gogoll et al. 2021 (ethics in development).

**Free resources:** free-for.dev (free hosting and tools); Storyset (illustrations).
