# Governance case seminar and reflection

How the Lecture 6 governance and ethics session was run, and what was expected:

- **Read the case in advance.** It was the Uber founder-CEO case. The session opened with someone retelling it, and the facilitator asked students to tell it as a story, from the beginning.
- **Group work in class.** Each group got its own prompts on Canvas (under Assignments). Each group had to prepare its answers, then present them in a few minutes. Groups were encouraged to use drawings, small graphs and arrows on their sheets, not just text.
- **Typical prompts:** Was the investors' demand legitimate governance or a power play? Were they protecting the company or their returns? Was the process ethical and fair? What would a transparent CEO-removal process look like? What are the arguments for and against founder control? What protections should minority shareholders have? What board composition and committees would give stronger oversight? Who is accountable when harassment becomes part of the culture?
- **Group reflection due one week later.** A template was provided on Canvas. The transcript suggests a length of about 1,500–2,000 words, but this was not fully clear in the recording, so check Canvas.
- Attendance was recorded.

The facilitator asked students to think about the case in terms of businesses they might run: which governance measures would they put in place?

## Use it in the capstone

The governance notes from this seminar feed the ACM paper's ethics discussion and our team agreement. Check Canvas for the reflection template and the deadline.
