# Needs assessment justifies features

The question raised in class: does automating something (such as auto-sorting a downloads folder) make users lazy? The lecturer's answer is to run a **needs assessment**, a structured check of what users actually need, before deciding.

Steps:

1. Do a **literature review**: what have others built, and what questions have users asked in the past?
2. **Engage stakeholders** and ask whether they want it.
3. If many people say they want it and it adds value, that is your justification to build it, whatever you personally think of it.

One caution: assume the people you consult are "sane", meaning a balanced sample whose stated wants are not distorted by pressure or extreme circumstances. Stated wants from a skewed group are not a sound basis for a feature.

## Use it in the capstone

Every major feature in the requirements document should trace back to evidence: a paper, a similar system, or a stakeholder interview. This is what makes the proposal's motivation and the paper's problem statement convincing.
