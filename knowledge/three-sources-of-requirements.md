# Three sources of requirements

There are usually three ways to collect data about a system for your research, and the lecturer wants them **in this order**:

1. **Literature review.** Read research papers in the domain, so you understand it as a developer, and extract requirements from them.
2. **Similar systems (related work).** Observe how others have built similar systems and what features they have, and extract requirements from those.
3. **Stakeholder engagement.** Only now meet the stakeholders.

Doing 1 and 2 first means you arrive prepared. When a stakeholder asks for a feature, you can say "that one is well known. Would you also consider this other feature?" If you go in empty-handed, ask "tell me what you think", and come back promising everything they said, some of it may not be feasible.

**With agile, you don't need exhaustive stakeholder engagement up front.** Constant engagement with clients during the sprints keeps surfacing ideas and priorities.

## Use it in the capstone

The requirements doc should cite all three sources for its key requirements. Plan stakeholder sessions after the literature and competitor scan, and log the follow-up engagements in sprint reviews.
