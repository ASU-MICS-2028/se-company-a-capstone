# Build and maintain against the requirements document

The lecturer compared software to building a house. All requirements go into the building plan; the contractor keeps going back to the plan while building. Building never happens separately from the requirements.

For software:
- **Implementation** follows the requirements systematically.
- **Design** analyses how well the requirements are being met for the users.
- **Testing** checks inputs and behaviour against the requirements.
- **Maintenance** relies on the requirements document too. When something breaks later, first ask: is the document up to date? If not, fixes get based on an old version.

So the requirements document should be **versioned** and kept current through the life of the system.

## Use it in the capstone

Keep our requirements doc under version control in the repo, and update it when sprints change scope. Note the version in sprint reviews.
