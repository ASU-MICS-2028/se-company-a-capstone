# Design pattern presentation format

In Lectures 10 to 12, each student claimed 2 or 3 Gang of Four patterns on a shared list of all 23 (ideally **one per family**: creational, structural, behavioural, then more behavioural ones once the smaller families ran out). They presented the patterns in class.

Each pattern needed three things:

1. **Overview:** describe the pattern and its intent.
2. **Diagram:** a UML or other diagram of its structure. The lecturer said the diagram and the application matter most.
3. **Practical example:** a real application of the pattern.

Don't copy the lecturer's own slides ("don't give me the same thing"). Strong presentations also contrasted similar patterns, gave the Big O of the approach, and stated trade-offs.

## Use it in the capstone

Use the same overview, diagram and example structure when we document each pattern in the architecture docs.
