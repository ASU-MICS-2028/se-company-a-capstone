# Agile contracts use milestones

Because agile values customer collaboration over contract negotiation, its contracts look different:

- Often the client's starting brief is vague ("help us build a system to support our economy"); they don't know the details yet.
- So the contract is **rolling**: a light agreement per **sprint** on what that sprint will deliver, rather than one long document fixing everything.
- Alternatively, a **parent contract** sets high-level **milestones** (month one: this product; month two: that one), and smaller agreements link back to it. The lecturer adds a buffer of about two months in case a milestone is missed.
- Have a **lawyer** involved either way: in agile, things change quickly and are easy to lose track of; in plan-driven projects, the documents are huge and need careful review.

## Use it in the capstone

Treat our capstone schedule as the parent contract (milestones to 18 December) and each sprint goal as the rolling agreement. Record agreed sprint deliverables in the Scrum records.
