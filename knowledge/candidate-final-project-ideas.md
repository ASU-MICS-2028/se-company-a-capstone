# Candidate final project ideas

Project directions the lecturer offered (teams may also bring their own):

- **A smart bamboo bike.** He is technical consultant to a Ghanaian bamboo-bicycle maker and wants to add technology to the bike. Ideas:
  - Pedal power stored in a battery, with a phone holder for charging.
  - A weight-sensing seat plus height entered at registration, giving **BMI** (body mass index) tracking.
  - Calories burned estimated from wheel revolutions, with targets and individual, team or class competitions (like a fitness-tracking app for cycling).
  - **Geofencing** (an alert when the bike leaves campus bounds), an annoying alarm, and a lock.
  He framed it as a healthy-lifestyle product, not just transport. Bikes cost about US$550 each, and only one was available at first.
- **Drones.** A visiting robotics engineer was lending drones (one for agriculture). Ideas included on-campus delivery. Engineering-lab staff have helped earlier classes with flight training and attaching embedded parts.
- **Smart vests** and a **health post** project (the lecturer was to send a reference link).
- Possible future work: submersibles, such as studying plastic pollution in water.

Expect help from engineering staff for hardware, and keep to what is realistic in one semester. A detailed scope document for the bike was promised.

## Use it in the capstone

Whatever problem Nyansapo Systems picks, it needs an AI element and an African context. If we choose hardware, plan the scope around one shared device and add simulation (see [test in simulation before you trust hardware](test-in-simulation-before-trusting-hardware.md)).
