# User research: survey, interviews, observation and competitor review

Issue: [#29](https://github.com/ASU-MICS-2028/se-company-a-capstone/issues/29)

## Goal

Run the Discover stage of the double diamond, so personas, requirements (#5) and designs (#6) rest on what real patients and staff say and do, not only on published studies. Rubric: Requirements and UX Design; the paper's Methodology.

## Done when

- [ ] Survey sent, with at least 20 responses
- [x] ~~6-12 interviews~~ Two interviews (P01, S01), notes in `docs/ux/research/interviews/`. We stopped at two: see [decision](../decisions/2026-11-23-stop-interviews-use-survey-and-literature.md)
- [x] At least one clinic staff interview (S01)
- [ ] Competitor review table
- [ ] `docs/ux/research/findings.md`: top needs, surprises, changes to personas and requirements

## Inputs and outputs

- **Uses:** the [survey draft](../docs/ux/research/survey.md), the [interview guide](../docs/ux/research/interview-guide.md), the proposal's personas
- **Produces:** `docs/ux/research/` (findings, anonymised notes, competitor table); input to #5 and #6

## Steps

1. Liza edits and approves the survey and interview guide (by 19 Nov)
2. Survey goes out in the class, family and community WhatsApp groups (19 Nov)
3. Each member books 1-2 interviews: market traders, workers, older relatives, carers; ideally one nurse or records clerk (19-22 Nov)
4. Optional: one early-morning observation at a polyclinic OPD, only from public waiting areas, no photos of people
5. Competitor review: GHS online booking, kiosk ticket systems, two or three others (20-21 Nov)
6. Synthesis session with the whole team: affinity-map the notes into needs (22 Nov)
7. Write `findings.md` and hand over to #5 and #6

## Out of scope

- Collecting any health information. We ask about waiting, not about illness.
- Recording audio without explicit consent.
