# Usability evaluation: expert review, walkthrough and user tests with SUS

Issue: [#30](https://github.com/ASU-MICS-2028/se-company-a-capstone/issues/30)

## Goal

Run the Deliver stage of the double diamond: test the Figma prototype, then the built screens, with experts and users, and fix the problems before the demo. This follows the three phases of Harte et al. 2017, cut down to fit our timeline. Results go into the ACM paper (#13).

## Done when

- [ ] Heuristic evaluation (Nielsen's 10) of the Figma prototype by 3+ teammates, problems rated by severity
- [ ] Cognitive walkthrough of three tasks: join by USSD, check my wait, staff calls next
- [ ] 3-5 think-aloud user tests with SUS; one on a button phone or the USSD simulator
- [ ] Problems logged as issues and fixed or deferred with a reason
- [ ] `docs/ux/evaluation/report.md`

## Inputs and outputs

- **Uses:** the Figma prototype (#6), personas and findings (#29), built screens (#10) for the second round
- **Produces:** `docs/ux/evaluation/` (report, SUS sheet, problem log); a Methodology and Results section for #13

## Steps

1. Prepare the task scripts and SUS form (by 28 Nov)
2. Heuristic evaluation and walkthrough as soon as the high-fidelity Figma is ready (about 2 Dec)
3. Round 1 user tests on the Figma prototype (Sprint 3)
4. Round 2 quick check on the built app before the 10 Dec demo
5. Write the report and hand the numbers to #13

## Out of scope

- Eye tracking and A/B tests
