# Choose the product and submit the concept note

Issue: [#1](https://github.com/ASU-MICS-2028/se-company-a-capstone/issues/1)

## Goal

Pick the one problem and intelligent system we will build, and get the instructor's approval. Nothing past this can be specific until it is settled. Rubric: Real-world Impact (5%), Innovative Use of Technologies (7%).

## Done when

- [x] Three to five candidate ideas compared in one table: problem, users, the intelligent part, data available, 6-week feasibility
- [x] Team vote recorded in `decisions/`
- [ ] Concept note submitted, and the instructor's feedback copied into the issue

## Inputs and outputs

- **Uses:** knowledge/ notes tagged `course` (brief, rubric, selection guidelines); our Prosit work (knowledge/ and learnings/ tagged `prior-work`)
- **Produces:** `decisions/<date>-product-choice.md`; concept note in `docs/proposal/`

## Steps

1. Everyone adds one or two ideas as comments on this issue
2. Score each against the five selection guidelines in the brief (real problem, intelligent technique, end-to-end SE, 6-week feasibility, room for analysis)
3. Vote, then write the decision file
4. Draft and submit the concept note

## Out of scope

- Detailed requirements (#5) and architecture (#7)
